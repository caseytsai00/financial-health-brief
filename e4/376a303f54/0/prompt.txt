  comparison date, and separately outstanding_balance on target date vs. outstanding_balance on comparison date — the same two-date comparison structure
  as gap 1's "Daily Posted Total Comparison," just applied to these two Revenue Snapshot metrics instead of Posted cash. And explicitly not combined into
  any single relationship or ratio ("without guessing or combining them") — two separate comparisons, not one computed metric.. And then in terms of what "collected revenue against outstanding balances" should actually show, The missing same day entry is an expected characteristic because revenue snapshot only has entries for 5 of the 9 days by design. Revenue snapshot does not update every day while the transaction ledger does. while the other is not based on any facts. Based on this information, I think it's fine to fall back to the most recent date because it's not the same as the forbidden rule rule regarding unknown amount and defaulting to zero.
